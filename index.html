Hi, I’m Alysia Hendley. As a stay-at-home mom, I took over managing my family’s finances and started helping family members with theirs. I know how overwhelming it can feel to work your way out of debt while keeping up with everyday life.

I have an associate degree in business and hope to pursue my bachelor’s. Blooming on a Budget grew from wanting to support others—especially moms and women who feel stuck with their money. Everyone is welcome here. This is practical budgeting support rooted in personal experience, not professional financial advice.

A little clarity

Wondering
where to start?

Do I need to have a budget already? +

No. You can start with a blank page, a budget that isn’t working for you, or simply a goal you want to work toward.

What can I get support with? +

The focus is everyday budgeting, savings, spending management, and planning for debt payoff. You can share which area feels most important to you.

Is this just for one type of person? +

Everyone is welcome. Alysia especially hopes to help moms and women who feel overwhelmed by spending or debt and want a supportive place to begin.

How do I begin? +

Fill in the short inquiry below, then open it in your texting app. Review it and tap Send to message Alysia. From there, you can discuss your goals, support options, and pricing.

Can we meet online or in person? +

Both options are available. You can also talk through messages if that feels more comfortable. Text Alysia to discuss availability and arrangements.
